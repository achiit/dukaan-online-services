About the Shop

One roof for forms, prints and books

I started this shop about five or six years ago because people in our area were travelling far for even small online work. A simple Aadhaar print or a caste certificate meant losing a whole day.

Today families bring me their documents and students come for books and copies. I explain what is needed, fill the form carefully and tell you the charge before I start.

If you are unsure about anything, just call or send a WhatsApp message. I will tell you what to bring.

  • All major online services in one place
  • Printing, photocopy and lamination while you wait
  • Students' books and stationery on the same counter
  • Simple guidance on documents before you travel
  • Printer, scanner, computer and lamination machine in the shop
